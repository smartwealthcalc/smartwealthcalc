Why Step-Up works

When your SIP rises with income, more money gets invested while the earlier contributions continue compounding. The effect becomes more visible over longer periods.

Compare before choosing

Use the normal SIP baseline alongside the Step-Up scenario so you can see both the additional contribution and the potential difference in projected corpus.

Use realistic assumptions

A higher assumed return increases the illustration, but it also increases uncertainty. Treat the result as a planning scenario, not a promise.
