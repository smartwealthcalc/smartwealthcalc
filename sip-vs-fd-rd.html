SIP vs RD

SIP is market-linked and returns are variable. RD generally uses a stated deposit rate subject to the bank's terms. A higher projected result is not a guarantee or a recommendation.

Inflation matters

Inflation-adjusted values are approximate purchasing power in today's rupees. Actual inflation and investment returns can differ substantially.
