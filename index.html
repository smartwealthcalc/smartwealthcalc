Why a step-up SIP can matter

A step-up plan increases your monthly contribution at regular intervals. If your income rises, increasing the amount invested may help you contribute more over time. In this calculator, the SIP rises once at the beginning of each investment year by your selected step-up percentage. Both contributions and projected growth are included in the final estimate.

Try it: compare 0% and 10% annual step-up using the same return and period.

Inflation and future purchasing power

Inflation means the same amount of money may buy fewer goods and services in the future. A projected corpus of ₹1 crore 20 years from now will not necessarily buy what ₹1 crore buys today. The real-value estimate discounts the future corpus using your selected annual inflation assumption.

Try it: change inflation from 0% to 6% to see the difference in today's money.

THE MATHEMATICS

How the estimate is calculated

For a fixed monthly investment with no step-up, the future value of an ordinary annuity is:

FV = P × [((1 + r)n − 1) / r]
  • P = monthly contribution
  • r = assumed monthly return (annual rate ÷ 12)
  • n = total number of monthly contributions

With annual step-up, the calculator projects each month's contribution using the annual increase and compounds it to the end of the selected period. Real purchasing power is estimated as future value ÷ (1 + inflation)years. Calculations are illustrative and do not account for taxes, expense ratios, exit loads or changing market returns.

LEARNING CENTRE

A few principles for long-term planning

Start with a goal

Estimate the amount and timeline you need, then test whether your contributions are realistic for your budget.

Keep assumptions humble

Returns vary. Test lower-return scenarios and avoid relying on a single projection for important decisions.

Review periodically

Revisit your plan as income, goals, time horizon and risk capacity change. Consider diversification and costs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does a SIP calculator work?

It estimates the future value of regular monthly investments using an assumed annual return. Actual market returns vary and are not guaranteed.

What is a step-up SIP?

A step-up SIP increases the monthly contribution at a chosen rate, often annually, as income changes over time. This calculator applies the increase at the start of each investment year.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns are market-linked. Calculator results are illustrations, not guarantees or predictions of future performance.

What does inflation-adjusted value mean?

It expresses a future amount in approximate today's purchasing power using an assumed annual inflation rate. Actual inflation may differ from your assumption.

Is my financial data uploaded?
